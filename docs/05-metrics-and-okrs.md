# Metrics and OKRs (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

> All targets are **hypotheses** invented for this practice document. There are no real baselines or results. A real team would measure a baseline first and then set targets with its data.

## North-star idea

New members get a correct first answer sooner, with moderators in control.

## Metric tree

| Level | Metric | Definition | Baseline | Hypothesis target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome | Median time to first answer | Question posted to first accepted answer | To measure | Lower than baseline |
| Outcome | New-member helpfulness rating | Share rating the answer helpful | To measure | Higher than baseline |
| Quality | Moderator acceptance rate | Approved (with or without edits) divided by drafts shown | To measure | Review in beta |
| Quality | Flag rate | Flagged divided by drafts shown | To measure | Keep low; investigate every flag |
| Coverage | No-suggestion rate | Questions with no draft divided by questions | To measure | Informs content audit |
| Guardrail | Drafts posted without approval | Count | 0 | Must remain 0 |
| Guardrail | Privacy incidents | Count | 0 | Must remain 0 |

## Sample OKRs (fictional)

### Objective 1: Moderators trust and control the drafts

| Key result | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| KR1 | Drafts posted without approval | 0 |
| KR2 | Pilot moderators who say drafts save them time (short survey) | Hypothesis: most of the pilot group |
| KR3 | Flagged drafts reviewed within one week | All of them |

### Objective 2: Faster, helpful first answers for new members

| Key result | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| KR1 | Median time to first answer in beta community | Lower than measured baseline |
| KR2 | Helpfulness rating in beta | At or above baseline |
| KR3 | Top ten missing topics turned into help articles | 10 |

## Measurement notes

- Measure a baseline for at least a few weeks before the beta.
- Compare like with like (same community, similar period).
- Watch for harm: faster is not better if answers are wrong.
- Small communities give noisy numbers; report counts alongside rates.
